It started with a mission, not a business.

A missionary we support has been serving in the hills of Nepal, in a community rebuilding after the floods.

Some of the farmers there came to faith.

Then they did the hard thing. They pulled out the drug crops they’d grown for years — and planted coffee.

A village coming back from the floods. New faith. New crop. Honest work. That coffee is what’s in the bottle.

India — House of Palms

House of Palms is a girls’ home in central India. The girls come from the Banchada community, one of the poorest and lowest-caste people groups in India, where for generations women and girls have been pushed into sexual exploitation and trafficking. House of Palms gives them a safe home, food, school, and job skills — a way out of a cycle their mothers and grandmothers couldn’t escape. Our own church, Living Stones, has walked with this ministry for years. Your bottle helps keep that home running.
